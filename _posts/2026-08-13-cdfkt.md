We added cdfkt into our pyKT package.

The link is [here](https://pykt-toolkit.readthedocs.io/en/latest/models.html#cdfkt) and the API is [here](https://pykt-toolkit.readthedocs.io/en/latest/pykt.models.html#module-pykt.models.cdfkt).

Original paper can be found at Wu et al. "Reducing Causal Attribution Ambiguity in Knowledge Tracing via Cognitive Decoupling and Adaptive Fusion." [Information Fusion (2026): 104648.
](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.inffus.2025.104648)

cdfkt reduces causal attribution ambiguity in knowledge tracing via cognitive decoupling and adaptive fusion. It decouples student cognitive state into situational adaptability (modeled by an exponential-decay kernel for short-term fluctuations) and long-range knowledge accumulation (modeled by a Mamba state-space model), then fuses them through an adaptive gate for response prediction.
